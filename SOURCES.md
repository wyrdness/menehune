# Menehune - Source Documentation

## Academic Sources

### Primary Ethnographic Works

1. **Beckwith, Martha Warren**. "Hawaiian Mythology." Yale University Press, 1940.
   - Comprehensive collection of Hawaiian myths including extensive Menehune traditions
   - Academic standard reference for Hawaiian folklore

2. **Luomala, Katharine**. "The Menehune of Polynesia and Other Mythical Little People of Oceania." Bernice P. Bishop Museum Bulletin 203, 1951.
   - Definitive academic study of Menehune
   - Comparative analysis with other Polynesian small people legends
   - Scholarly examination of origins and cultural significance

3. **Westervelt, W.D.** "Hawaiian Legends of Old Honolulu." G.H. Doran Company, 1915.
   - Early collection of Hawaiian legends
   - Includes multiple Menehune stories and place associations

4. **Fornander, Abraham**. "An Account of the Polynesian Race: Its Origins and Migrations." Charles E. Tuttle Company, 1969 (reprint of 1878-1885 original).
   - Historical documentation of Hawaiian traditions
   - Genealogies mentioning Menehune connections

5. **Malo, David**. "Hawaiian Antiquities (Moolelo Hawaii)." Bernice P. Bishop Museum, 1951 (written 1830s-1840s).
   - Native Hawaiian historian's account
   - Pre-contact traditions preserved

### Modern Hawaiian Scholarship

6. **Pukui, Mary Kawena, and Caroline Curtis**. "Hawaii Island Legends." Kamehameha Schools Press, 1996.
   - Native Hawaiian scholar's collection
   - Cultural context and interpretation

7. **Kamakau, Samuel M.** "The Works of the People of Old." Bernice P. Bishop Museum Special Publication 61, 1976.
   - Native Hawaiian historian (19th century)
   - Traditional knowledge documentation

8. **Kame'eleihiwa, Lilikala**. "Native Land and Foreign Desires." Bishop Museum Press, 1992.
   - Modern Native Hawaiian scholarly perspective
   - Cultural interpretation of traditions

9. **Pukui, Mary Kawena, E.W. Haertig, and Catherine A. Lee**. "Nana I Ke Kumu (Look to the Source)." Hui Hanai, 1972.
   - Hawaiian cultural values and beliefs
   - Context for understanding Menehune in Hawaiian worldview

10. **Charlot, John**. "The Hawaiian Poetry of Religion and Politics." University of Hawaii Press, 1985.
    - Analysis of Hawaiian traditional literature
    - Religious and cultural context

## Archaeological and Engineering Studies

11. **Kikuchi, William K.** "Prehistoric Hawaiian Fishponds." Science 193(4250): 295-299, 1976.
    - Scientific study of structures attributed to Menehune
    - Engineering analysis

12. **Apple, Russell A., and William K. Kikuchi**. "Ancient Hawaii Shore Zone Fishponds." Hawaiian National Park, 1975.
    - Detailed documentation of fishpond construction
    - Includes 'Alekoko (Menehune Fishpond)

13. **Kirch, Patrick Vinton**. "Feathered Gods and Fishhooks: An Introduction to Hawaiian Archaeology and Prehistory." University of Hawaii Press, 1985.
    - Archaeological overview of ancient Hawaii
    - Context for attributed structures

14. **Kirch, Patrick V.** "How Chiefs Became Kings: Divine Kingship and the Rise of Archaic States in Ancient Hawai'i." University of California Press, 2010.
    - Ancient Hawaiian society and labor organization
    - Context for large construction projects

15. **McAllister, J. Gilbert**. "Archaeology of Oahu." Bernice P. Bishop Museum Bulletin 104, 1933.
    - Early archaeological survey
    - Documentation of sites with Menehune associations

16. **Hommon, Robert J.** "The Ancient Hawaiian State: Origins of a Political Society." Oxford University Press, 2013.
    - Ancient Hawaiian political structure
    - Organization of labor for major projects

17. **Cordy, Ross**. "Exalted Sits the Chief: The Ancient History of Hawai'i Island." Mutual Publishing, 2000.
    - Island-specific archaeological history
    - Traditional sites and their construction

18. **Kolb, Michael J.** "Monumentality and the Rise of Religious Authority in Pre-Contact Hawaii." Current Anthropology 47(5): 807-812, 2006.
    - Analysis of large-scale Hawaiian construction
    - Labor mobilization in ancient society
